# Support

**Bug reports, questions and ideas:** open an issue at
[github.com/SeasonalPugForecast/whether/issues](https://github.com/SeasonalPugForecast/whether/issues). A free
GitHub account is needed to post.

Please include the app version, your iOS version, and what you expected to happen. Leave out API
keys and your exact address.

**Email:** [pugforecast@gmail.com](mailto:pugforecast@gmail.com) if you would rather not use
GitHub.

**Security problems:** see [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/SeasonalPugForecast/whether/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
Please do not post them as public issues.

See the [privacy policy]({{ site.baseurl }}/privacy/) for what the app sends and where.
